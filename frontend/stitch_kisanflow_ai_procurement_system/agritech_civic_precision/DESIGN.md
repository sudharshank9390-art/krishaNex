## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a balance between sovereign public infrastructure and agile agricultural intelligence. Designed for nationwide grain procurement centers (mandis), logistics coordinators, and rural farmers, the system must project institutional authority, uncompromised clarity, and high-tech utility under adverse outdoor lighting conditions.

The visual style blends **Corporate/Modern Civic Design** with **Tactile Utility**:
- **Clarity under Sunlight:** High contrast, legible typography, and decisive structural grouping ensure readability across affordable mobile devices used in open procurement yards.
- **Dual-Audience Architecture:** A unified design language that scales from high-density, multi-column operational command boards to high-touch, thumb-friendly mobile cards for farmers tracking tokens and queue positions.
- **Calm, Authoritative Tone:** Replacing chaotic agricultural logistical friction with predictable, systematic calm through grounding forest greens, crisp metric containers, and unmistakable status signifiers.

## Colors

The palette grounds modern computational intelligence in agricultural civic credibility. 

### Core Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#15803d` - Deep Forest Emerald):** The bedrock of institutional trust. Used for top application chrome, primary interactive actions, verified procurement states, and official seals.
- **Secondary (`#22c55e` - Vibrant Leaf Green):** Represents flow, positive completion, and active real-time progression. Used for low-congestion indicators, active queue progress tracks, and live sync pulses.
- **Tertiary (`#0ea5e9` - AI Precision Cyan):** Signals predictive intelligence, slot optimization algorithms, automated tonnage estimation, and KisanFlow AI recommendations.
- **Neutral Surface & Dark Slate (`#0f172a`):** Used for primary typography, tabular data headers, and high-emphasis metric displays.
- **Canvas & Surface Backgrounds:** 
  - Page Canvas: `#f8faf5` (warm botanical cream tint) to eliminate the glare of harsh white screens in field environments.
  - Surface Containers: Pure `#ffffff` for elevated cards, nested cleanly against `#f0fdf4` (mint tint) for active slot panels.

### Queue & Congestion Alert Semantics
- **Low Congestion / Smooth Flow:** Background `#dcfce7`, Border `#86efac`, Text `#14532d`
- **Moderate Congestion / Delay Warning:** Background `#fef3c7`, Border `#fcd34d`, Text `#78350f`, Accent `#f59e0b`
- **High Congestion / Bottleneck Alert:** Background `#fee2e2`, Border `#fca5a5`, Text `#7f1d1d`, Accent `#ef4444`
- **AI Glowing Tints:** Subtle cyan-emerald aura (`rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.12)`) reserved strictly for machine-predicted scheduling and dynamic token allocation badges.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes extreme legibility, standardized public service compliance, and technical precision.

- **Public Sans (Display & Body):** Developed for rigorous public accessibility. It provides optical weight distribution, distinct letterforms (e.g., distinguishing uppercase `I`, lowercase `l`, and digit `1`), and superior performance across multilingual transliteration environments.
- **Space Grotesk (Labels, Tokens & Data Readouts):** Infuses digital telemetry into operational figures. Used exclusively for queue token numbers (`#K-408`), procurement yard codes (`MANDI-WB-09`), tonnage figures (`4.8 MT`), and time-slot countdowns.
- **Accessibility Safeguards:** Avoid font sizes below 12px anywhere in the product. Numbers in data-dense tables must use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during live queue updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The system relies on a responsive, content-aware fluid layout tailored to distinct operational contexts.

### Grid Infrastructure
- **Desktop (Command Dashboard / 1024px+):** 12-column grid with 24px gutters and max-width clamping at 1440px. Optimized for split views: real-time intake telemetry on the left (8 cols) and predictive AI dispatch queues on the right (4 cols).
- **Tablet (Mandi Weighbridge Operator / 768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with 24px gutters. Stacked analytical modules with persistent quick-action status bars.
- **Mobile (Farmer Queue View / <768px):** 4-column layout with 16px margins. Single-column linear flow prioritizing the immediate token pass, gate direction, estimated arrival time, and SMS/IVR sync state.

### Touch Ergonomics
All interactive triggers in mobile farmer modes maintain a minimum target dimension of 48px × 48px to accommodate one-handed operation in transit or agricultural workspaces.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid optical fatigue and preserve contrast under sunlight, depth is rendered via crisp structural outlines and soft, diffused environmental ambient shading rather than heavy artificial shadows.

- **Base Level (Canvas):** Flat `#f8faf5` background with no elevation.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Metric Tiles):** `#ffffff` fill framed by a crisp border (`1px solid #e2e8f0`) and an ambient tinted drop: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0px 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Floating Status, Gate Direction Modals):** White card framed by `#cbd5e1` with extended shadow: `0px 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **AI Highlight Elevation:** For AI-recommended time slots and live bottleneck re-routing cards, augment Elevation 1 with an inner highlight stroke: `inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.4)` and a glowing perimeter blur `0px 0px 16px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.15)`.
- **Alert States:** Elevate critical high-congestion banners using a high-visibility perimeter: `inset 0 0 0 1px #fca5a5` over a crisp 1px `#ef4444` structural base.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** shape language (`roundedness: 2` with standard radii of 8px/0.5rem, containers at 16px/1rem, and hero cards at 24px/1.5rem).

- **Data Tiles & Cards:** Standard `16px` (`rounded-lg`) border radius creates an approachable, modern feel without sacrificing layout density in tabular dashboards.
- **Status Badges & Tokens:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`) roundedness is reserved strictly for ephemeral indicators: queue badges, congestion meters, and gate assignment pills.
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** `8px` (`rounded`) corner radius provides stable geometric anchoring for numerical inputs, license plate verification, and crop moisture metrics.
- **Inner Nested Elements:** Nested children strictly follow the outer-radius minus border-padding rule to eliminate visual disharmony between card wrappers and nested image/data frames.

- **Container:** Height 48px, background `#ffffff`, border `1.5px solid #cbd5e1`, text `#0f172a`, placeholder `#94a3b8`.
- **State Changes:** Active/focus border turns to `#15803d` with an accessible `0 0 0 3px rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.15)` ring. Error border `#ef4444`.
- **Numeric Weighment Input:** High-legibility monospaced digits with right-aligned unit affixes (`Quintals`, `Tons`, `Moisture %`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Geometry:** 20px × 20px square with 4px corner radius for checkboxes; 20px round for radios.
- **Colors:** Default border `2px solid #94a3b8`. Selected state fills `#15803d` with white check/dot icon. High contrast ratio guaranteed against card backgrounds.
